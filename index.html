    About this model

    • Random forest, 200 trees, trained on 700 applications from the UCI German Credit dataset and tested on the other 300.
    • Trained with balanced class weights, so scores rank risk well but are not calibrated default probabilities.
    • Group results use 300 test applications. Groups under 20 are marked and not flagged.
    • Age and a sex-linked status field are direct model inputs. A production review would test removing them and check for proxies.
